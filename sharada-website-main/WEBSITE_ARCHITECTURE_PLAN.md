# Sharada Enterprise Website Architecture Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

This website should be treated as a growth platform, not just a marketing site. The company operates across three high-value domains: Technology, Marketing, and Media & Production. The architecture should therefore support three outcomes at once:

- Trust-building for enterprise and mid-market buyers
- Lead generation for service inquiries and project bookings
- Long-term scalability for content, portals, CRM, and internal operations

The current site is visually strong but still limited for an enterprise-grade agency. It needs a stronger information architecture, dedicated service and portfolio systems, SEO and content infrastructure, and business workflows such as lead capture, quote requests, case studies, careers, and client portal experiences.

## 2. Current Audit of the Existing Site

### What is already present
- Strong visual identity and premium aesthetics
- Core landing page structure
- Contact CTA and modal
- Basic service and location sections
- FAQ and newsletter block
- AI Copilot experience

### What is missing or underdeveloped
- Dedicated service detail pages for each major offering
- Portfolio and case-study system
- Blog and thought leadership architecture
- Careers and application funnel
- Legal and policy architecture
- Client portal and employee portal experiences
- Admin/content management layer
- SEO content architecture and schema planning
- CRM/lead management workflow
- Security, analytics, and accessibility implementation plan
- Clear conversion paths for different buyer types

### Architectural conclusion
The site should evolve from a polished one-page marketing experience into a modular, multi-layered growth platform with:

- Public marketing pages for brand trust and lead generation
- Business systems for leads, proposals, projects, and onboarding
- Content systems for SEO, resources, and storytelling
- Internal operational portals for clients, employees, and administrators

### URL structure guidance
- Use clean, human-readable URLs.
- Prefer `/services/web-development` over query-based or ID-based URLs.
- Prefer `/case-studies/<slug>` rather than `/projects/123`.
- Keep category pages under singular route conventions.

### Parent and child pages
- Parent pages should host grouped content and link to children.
- Child pages should be deeply optimized for specific terms.

### Internal linking strategy
- Link each service page to a related case study.
- Link each case study to its relevant service page.
- Link blog posts to service pages and portfolio items.
- Link portfolio items to related industries and services.
- Use contextual CTA blocks rather than repetitive footer-only CTAs.

### Navigation flow
1. Awareness: Home -> Services -> Industries -> Portfolio
2. Consideration: Case Studies -> Blog -> About
3. Conversion: Request Proposal -> Book Consultation -> Contact
4. Retention: Client Portal -> Support -> Resources

### User journey
- Startup founder sees the homepage and explores services.
- Marketing leader compares case studies and pricing guidance.
- Decision-maker fills a proposal request form.
- Internal teams onboard through the client portal.
